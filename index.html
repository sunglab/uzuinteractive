About us

A small studio with a quiet mission.

Uzu means “swirl” — the gentle spiral of water, wind and breath. It's how we think about wellbeing: not a destination, but a rhythm you return to.

UZU INTERACTIVE INC. is a Canadian company building wellness apps for iOS and Android. Our team has spent two decades in real-time graphics and mobile engineering, and has shipped apps enjoyed by tens of millions of people worldwide. Now we're putting that craft toward something simple: helping people rest.
