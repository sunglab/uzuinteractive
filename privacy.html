Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

In short: our apps do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile applications published by UZU INTERACTIVE INC. (“UZU INTERACTIVE”, “we”, “us”), a company based in Ontario, Canada, including our sleep and meditation apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play (each, an “App”).

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, our Apps do not:

  • require you to create an account or sign in;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts;
  • collect your location, health data, or device identifiers for tracking;
  • use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs;
  • track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and preferences — for example, favourite sounds, timer lengths, or session history — are stored only on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. You can delete it at any time by deleting the App.

If you have iCloud or device backups enabled, this on-device data may be included in backups managed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We have no access to those backups.

Purchases

If an App offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your payment details. We may receive anonymous confirmation that a purchase was made so the App can unlock content.

Children’s privacy

Because our Apps do not collect personal information from anyone, they do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Your rights

Since we do not collect or hold personal information about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. This reflects our commitments under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and similar laws elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how our Apps handle information — for example, by adding an optional feature that requires data — we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact UZU INTERACTIVE INC. at admin@uzuinteractive.com.
